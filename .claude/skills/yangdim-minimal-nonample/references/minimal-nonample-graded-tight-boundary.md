# Graded replacement tight-boundary packet

Load this packet for canonical tight supports, strict-defect descendants, and finite transition/tangent-frontier bookkeeping in the old low-degree extenture route.

## Tight support closure

- `lem:canonical_tight_support_descent_or_top`: for the canonical tight support `P_can=L union T union V`, a non-shattered deletion gives a positive-height extenture of size between `m+1` and `VC(H)` with strictly smaller off-`K` defect; full boundary shattering forces a top punctured/Claim-X or antipodal co-pair endpoint.
- `cor:canonical_top_endpoint_overlap_contradiction`: the apparent full-boundary top branch contradicts the maximal-overlap choice of the shattered set `K`.
- `cor:defect_minimal_tight_branch_copair` now records an exclusion: if no positive-height low-degree extenture has smaller off-`K` defect, the tight branch cannot survive.
- `cor:canonical_copair_uses_all_v` is retained only for formal/non-normalized top endpoints; in the normalized tight equations it forces `m=VC(H)`.

## Strict-defect descendants

- `def:strict_defect_descendant` and `cor:canonical_strict_defect_endpoint`: every canonical tight branch has a strict-defect descendant `E` with `m<|E|<=k` and `0<|E\K|<|D\K|`.
- `cor:canonical_unit_defect_descent_terminal`: unit off-`K` defect is terminal; flipping the unique off-`K` coordinate gives a fibre projecting onto all of `K\E`.
- `lem:low_degree_extenture_flip_cover`: non-empty flip fibres over the off-`K` block cover all of the outside shattered block.
- `lem:defect_minimal_endpoint_failure_preserves_defect`: at a defect-minimal higher-defect endpoint, any low-degree first-failure lift inside a non-empty flip fibre must keep every remaining off-`K` coordinate.
- `cor:endpoint_minimal_failure_exchange_profile`: after minimizing support among defect-minimal descendants, low-degree fibre failures split into same-size endpoint exchanges and positive-height defect-preserving lifts.

## Finite transition and tangent frontier

- `cor:weighted_endpoint_exchange_obstruction`: secondary weight normalization makes height-zero endpoint exchanges weight-nonincreasing; weight-increasing replacements must become positive-height defect-preserving lifts.
- `cor:endpoint_exchange_frontier`: unseparated endpoint exchanges lie in an explicit tangent cone generated by endpoint-minimal overlap sets.
- `cor:top_endpoint_no_positive_height` and `cor:top_endpoint_exchange_frontier`: top endpoints have no positive-height low-degree fibre failures; top residuals lie on the top tangent frontier.
- `cor:defect_minimal_support_interval`: same-defect first-failure lifts stay inside the interval of support sizes realized by minimum off-`K` defect descendants.
- `cor:endpoint_transition_graph`: low-degree endpoint transitions form a finite labelled directed graph; strict one-sign height chains terminate, and one-sign cycles are height zero.

## Status

This packet isolates finite bookkeeping. It does not prove the residual frontier empty by itself; emptiness required the later global VC-radius/extenture-purity proof.
