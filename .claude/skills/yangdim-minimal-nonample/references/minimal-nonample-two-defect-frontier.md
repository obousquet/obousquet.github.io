# Two-defect frontier and flip-fibre handoff packet

Load this packet for the old two-defect branch: flip-fibre covers, punctured-square handoffs, strict-drop endpoints, and direct-exchange/lateral-drift residuals.

## Flip-fibre cover and boundary

- `cor:single_flip_fibers_contain_k_edges`: for every `u in U=D\K`, the single-flip fibre `H_{D=phi^u}` is nonempty and ample, and strongly shatters each singleton `{x}` with `x in V=K\D`.
- `cor:nonempty_flip_fibers_cover_v`: projections of non-empty flip fibres `H_{D=phi^T}` to `V`, over non-empty `T subset U`, cover `2^V`.
- `cor:minimal_flip_boundary_extentures`: if `T subset U` minimally realizes a `V`-label, then every extenture below a missing predecessor must use a coordinate of `T\T'`.
- `cor:two_defect_double_flip_boundary`: in the double-flip-covered two-defect branch, pairwise size-`m+1` lifts deleting the other off-`K` coordinate are impossible.
- `cor:two_defect_punctured_square_handoff`: the exact local handoff has one single-flip projection `2^W\{alpha}` while the double-flip fibre realizes the puncture.

## Residual profiles and strict drops

- `cor:two_defect_pairwise_residual_profiles`: minimum exchange deletes two coordinates of `R`, a size-`m+1` lift deletes either a coordinate of `R` or the already-flipped coordinate, and strict off-`K` defect drop only deletes the already-flipped coordinate.
- `cor:already_flipped_deletion_cross_support`: the already-flipped deletion profile is a size-`m` cross-support-purity obstruction, not a separate higher-lift phenomenon.
- `cor:already_flipped_graded_or_top`: the branch is either graded with `m<VC(H)` or top over the outside block when `m=VC(H)`.
- `cor:top_strict_drop_trichotomy`: the top case is routed to unique-puncture/Claim-X or antipodal co-pair alternatives.
- `lem:unit_defect_extenture_full_fiber` and `cor:graded_strict_drop_terminal_unit_defect`: the graded strict-drop case is terminal unit defect with a full flipped fibre on the remaining outside block.
- `cor:two_defect_strict_drop_endpoint`: strict drops are either known top alternatives or terminal unit-defect data.
- `cor:two_defect_residuals_defect_preserving`: after excluding strict drops, remaining pairwise lifts preserve both original off-`K` coordinates.
- `cor:complete_two_defect_pairwise_reduction`: every lift is a weighted nonincreasing minimum exchange, a routed strict-drop endpoint, or a finite-height defect-preserving higher lift.

## Exchange frontier

- `cor:two_defect_exchange_frontier`: after secondary weight normalization, a failed pair that beats every old two-subset must enter an endpoint or higher-lift branch.
- `cor:two_defect_exchange_frontier_dual`: the residual pair lies in a tangent cone generated by maximum-overlap minimum supports.
- `cor:two_defect_frontier_local_certificate`: any residual pair admits a local fractional replacement certificate.
- `cor:two_defect_frontier_replacement_types`: positive generators are one-coordinate exchanges or direct two-coordinate exchanges.
- `cor:two_defect_frontier_integral_witness`: tangent-cone containment is equivalent to an actual direct two-exchange or paired one-exchanges.
- `cor:two_defect_frontier_exchange_closure`: if paired one-exchanges force a direct two-exchange, then the frontier reduces to direct two-exchange existence.
- `cor:paired_one_exchange_frontier_artifact` and `cor:fixed_trace_frontier_direct_only`: paired one-exchanges are proof-method artifacts for the fixed missing trace; actual minimum-size fixed-trace extentures have direct two-exchange support.

## Drift and label-mismatch taxonomy

- `cor:external_one_exchange_support_or_label_mismatch`: an external one-exchange either changes off-`K` support or has a label mismatch with the fixed trace.
- `cor:one_exchange_support_drift_normal_form`: support drift is lateral off-`K` exchange at fixed size and fixed overlap, not immediate descent.
- `cor:one_exchange_label_mismatch_normal_form` and `cor:one_exchange_label_mismatch_old_coordinate`: fixed-support label mismatch must flip an old coordinate, not only the inserted coordinate.
- `cor:one_exchange_residual_taxonomy`: residuals are lateral off-`K` drift or fixed-support old-coordinate label flips.
- `cor:lateral_drift_rerooting`: lateral drift re-roots the normalized support relative to the same shattered set `K`; it is not a same-`K` descent by itself.
