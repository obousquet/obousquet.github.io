# Alexander-dual algebra and VC-one exact stratum

Use this packet for `I_{Delta_H}`, componentwise linearity, Alexander-dual shellability/SCM, linear quotients, and vertex decomposability.

## Main classifications

- The componentwise-linear target is the mixed-degree Stanley-Reisner ideal `I_{Delta_H}`, equivalently SCM of the Alexander-dual complex `Delta_H^*`. This is transverse to ampleness.
- For `|X|>=2`, the full cube is ample/CM but `I_{Delta_H}` is a quadratic complete intersection with a minimal degree-4 Koszul first syzygy; it is not componentwise linear.
- If `Delta_H` is flag / `T(H)` is 2-Helly, `I_{Delta_H}` is the edge ideal of the graph of inconsistent literal pairs. Componentwise linearity then reduces to chordality of the compatible-pair graph by Froberg. This only sees 2-local data.
- Componentwise linearity of `I_{Delta_H}` forces `VC(H)<=1`; a shattered pair gives a minimal degree-4 Koszul obstruction in the degree-2 strand.
- `thm:componentwise_linear_vc_one`: `I_{Delta_H}` has linear quotients in nondecreasing degree order iff `VC(H)<=1`. Equivalently, `Delta_H^*` is shellable/SCM in the standard non-pure sense iff `VC(H)<=1`.
- `thm:vc_one_dual_vertex_decomposable`: `I_{Delta_H}` is vertex-splittable iff `Delta_H^*` is vertex-decomposable iff `VC(H)<=1`.

## Linear resolution classification

For nonempty `H` on nonempty `X`, `I_{Delta_H}` has a linear resolution iff either:

- `H` is a singleton, giving a 1-linear ideal generated by absent literals; or
- `H` has no constant coordinate, `T(H)` is 2-Helly, and the compatible-pair graph is chordal, giving the 2-linear flag case.

## Proof route guardrails

- The reverse VC-one theorem uses the rooted-tree/laminar-cut characterization: after fixing a root `h0`, the cuts `L_x={h:h(x)!=h0(x)}` are laminar.
- The proof orders absent singletons first, then quadratic circuits in co-chordal order, then cover circuits by degree/sourcelex.
- The non-flag example `H={110,101,011}` remains useful: the absent trace `111` gives a cubic cover circuit, so the reverse theorem is not only Froberg/chordality.
- The vertex-decomposable proof is closed in `main.tex`; the old private-literal and one-point basic-link reductions are proof history. Do not reopen them unless auditing `drafts/vc_one_dual_vertex_decomposable_route.tex`.
- The one-inclusion graph alone is insufficient for arbitrary VC-one classes: it forgets repeated labels and may be disconnected for non-ample VC-one classes.
