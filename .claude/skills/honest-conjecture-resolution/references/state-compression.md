# Strategy Reviews, Progress, And Campaign State

Use this reference at route selection, a plateau, or a methodology audit. The main skill owns the
review trigger. Keep the review in the existing ledger; do not create another tracking system.

## What Counts As Progress

Assess mathematical correctness, contribution to the conjecture, and broader mathematical value
separately. Outcomes can overlap; reusable theory is substantive research, not merely local activity.

In a result summary, separate the contribution (which statement or capability changed), confidence
(author proof, independent audit, exact finite certificate, numerical evidence, or unresolved),
and effect on the target or theory objective. A completed assignment is a separate task status.
An audit can increase confidence without changing the contribution or last-discovery date. Accept
only the audited statement and version; formal verification also requires checking that the encoded
statement matches the intended target.

| Outcome | Evidence required | Consequence |
|---------|-------------------|-------------|
| Original target advance | An essential obligation is discharged, a sufficient proof route loses a substantive obstacle, requested coverage expands, or the requested bound improves. State the implication. | Update the last advance toward that target and remaining obligations. |
| Reusable theory advance | A general construction, operation, invariant, comparison, or structural theorem supplies a new capability or understanding for a natural class of objects. Give its scope and evidence of value. | Record a research achievement and consider further theory development; report the conjecture's status separately. |
| Decision-changing learning | A credible live route is excluded or becomes better justified; an exact reformulation makes a specified essential step more accessible. State the decision that changes. | Revise route selection; keep the time since the last goal advance visible. |
| Local result | A correct theorem, example, or diagnostic with no established goal effect, broader theory value, or decision effect. | Preserve it at appropriate fidelity; it does not renew the route automatically. |
| Verification or maintenance | Evidence is checked, a bug fixed, or records made accurate. | Do required correctness work. Do not present it as a new mathematical advance. |

When the user's research scope includes general tools and theory, pursue emerging theory objectives
within that scope without requiring a known implication back to the conjecture or renewed permission.
Keep both objectives visible; a theory achievement does not claim the conjecture is resolved. Respect
a narrowly specified task or budget rather than silently broadening it. A universal impossibility
theorem about a specified method may change strategy; another failure of a method already excluded
usually does not.
A general classification need not be a short list. Judge completion by the user's requested form,
quantifiers, admissibility, and coverage, not by an easier surrogate such as recognition.

## Select Work Backward From The Goal

At a strategy checkpoint, compare a small number of genuinely different approaches. For each, ask:

- If it succeeds, what remains between its conclusion and the conjecture, or what general
  mathematical capability or structural understanding will it establish?
- Why should its hardest indispensable claim be true? Use a solved analogue, structural reason,
  discriminating examples, or an actual proof argument rather than convenience alone.
- What is the cheapest experiment, proof attempt, or exact literature check that could change the
  decision to invest? Check the input's eligibility before starting dependent work.
- What is the likely scope of the payoff, the main failure risk, and the cost of the next useful
  evidence? Use qualitative comparisons; avoid fictitious probabilities or numerical scores.

A special case is worth choosing when it isolates an essential mechanism, supplies a transfer lemma,
or tests competing explanations. Specify what would justify returning to the general problem. A
larger catalogue or easier auxiliary statement is not preferable merely because it can be finished.
For an expanding family, identify what still lies outside it and why the next extension addresses
that gap. Unbounded dimension or more parameters alone does not establish structural exhaustiveness.

A classification route should distinguish sound construction from exhaustive recovery and intrinsic
admissibility. Other goals need their own completion obligations. Do not replace an essential unknown
by an assumption and count the resulting reformulation as its resolution.

## Develop Intermediate Theory Objectives

During a proof attempt, notice when the objects lack a useful calculus, operation, representation,
invariant, or comparison theorem. Developing it can become an intermediate research objective even
if it is neither a special case of the conjecture nor an established prerequisite for its proof.
Give it a precise mathematical target and judge it on its own merits as well as its possible use
for the original problem. These opportunities can arise before a route stalls.

Look for evidence of value such as:

- **Natural scope:** the definitions address a structurally defined class and its natural operations,
  rather than encoding the exceptions in one construction. An infinite family alone is not enough.
- **New capability or understanding:** a construction, composition law, duality, normal form, or
  comparison permits arguments or explains phenomena that were previously inaccessible or ad hoc.
- **Mathematical substance:** nontrivial structural consequences, a unification of distinct phenomena,
  or useful relations to existing theory; check what is new against the relevant literature.
- **Prospects for reuse:** concrete potential uses beyond the initiating subcase. Separate demonstrated
  uses from plausible ones; do not demand multiple completed applications or an unrelated field.

These are grounds for judgment, not a checklist every nascent theory must already pass. During
discovery, examples and partial laws may justify further development before the structural theorem
is available. Set the next milestone in terms of capability, scope, or understanding. At review,
ask what makes that theory more promising or substantial, even if the conjecture has not moved.
Neither attractive terminology nor routine lemmas about a bespoke object justify indefinite renewal.

A promising theory may justify a distinct active branch and ultimately a full journal paper. State
the branch's central mathematical question, natural scope, prospective main results, and relationship
to existing work. Its contribution can be a substantial structural understanding of the objects;
applications elsewhere strengthen the case but need not already exist. Assess publication potential
by coherence, novelty, depth, and generality, not by whether the original conjecture is solved.
An early branch proposal may give candidate theorems and open dependencies rather than a finished
paper. Within the user's endorsed research scope, develop it without repeatedly seeking approval;
keep its milestones and the original conjecture's status distinct. A full theory paper is a valid
campaign outcome, while completion of the original conjecture still requires its own proof.

## Review The Route, Not Just The Latest Lemma

Persist the route's mechanism, preserved information, effort since review, and reopening condition
across turns and compactions. Renaming a construction, changing a parameter, refining a counterexample,
or switching external terminology does not create a new route when the same obstacle remains.

At the agreed checkpoint (default: four substantive attempts), inspect cumulative results across the
campaign as well as within its routes, even if all attempts produced a lemma. Switching routes does
not restart this counter. Review earlier after a decisive refutation, repeated identical failure,
or an exhausted effort allowance. Ask:

1. What changed in the conjecture's completion obligations, and what reusable theory was developed?
2. What new evidence makes this route more promising, or its central step more tractable?
3. Are we advancing a sufficient proof route or a worthwhile general theory objective, or only
   optimizing a proxy or special case?
4. Would we choose this next step if the previous investment were zero?
5. Is continuing better justified than a different mechanism or a fresh derivation from the target?

End the review with an actionable choice and use it in the next assignment:

- Continue: name what made the indispensable step more tractable and the next informative test.
- Change approach: name the new operation or structural input, and how it changes the unresolved
  implication. A renamed formulation is insufficient by itself.
- Pursue independent theory: state the mathematical question being funded on its own merits and
  keep its connection to the conjecture explicit, including when unknown.
- Hold: preserve the results and specify what evidence would justify reopening.

Do not renew solely because there is another available lemma or family extension. Long, difficult
proofs may deserve sustained investment: explicit progress inside their indispensable step, a newly
applicable theorem, or a demonstrated new capability can justify it before a goal advance. State
that evidence and the next review point. Keep cumulative effort against the same missing implication
when notation, workers, or route names change. Analogous gaps in different targets are not thereby
proved equivalent or equally hard.

Do not enforce literal deletion of an old lemma for every new idea. Necessary proof development can
increase the number of intermediate claims. The question is whether the dependencies become better
understood and more tractable, not whether a dashboard has fewer nodes.

## Separate Discovery From Commitment

Use a bounded discovery pass when a promising theory opportunity arises or no credible route is
available. It may examine unfamiliar methods, rederive a solved neighbouring problem, change the
formulation of the whole target, or construct toy
models. It need not produce a theorem on every turn. At the end, select one promising mechanism for
an adversarial check, or record that the pass was inconclusive. Do not dress inconclusive exploration
up as a new counterexample or a proliferation of conditional lemmas.

A new framework should make a concrete prediction, expose a usable comparison or operation, unify
phenomena, or identify a worthwhile structural problem. Assess its first relevant consequence before
funding substantial additional machinery. A missing transfer lemma can be the original difficulty in disguise: explain why it is
now more tractable before treating it as a promising reduction.

## Restart From A Short Problem Packet

If reviews repeatedly end at the same obstacle, stop using the latest local lemma as the default
starting point. Prepare a short packet containing:

- the original problem and completion standard;
- definitions and the strongest relevant proved results;
- a few examples and counterexamples that distinguish plausible mechanisms;
- links to exact proofs and source theorems, with their hypotheses;
- the core unresolved implication.

Keep one current packet in the existing ledger opening or handoff, with a searchable result map
and links to proof owners. Rewrite current summaries in place; archive chronology rather than
copying it into each packet. Retrieve historical detail when it tests a concrete question or catches
a known failure. Bound repeated failed index calls and use direct sources when retrieval is
unavailable; `research-knowledge` gives the recovery procedure.

Temporarily set aside the chronological route narrative. Derive alternative proof architectures from
this packet, including the nearest solved analogue: locate the step that made that problem tractable
and test whether it transfers. Consult the archive afterward to catch known failures. This reduces
anchoring; it does not erase negative evidence or justify repeating old work. In a single conversation,
this is a deliberate rereading exercise, not a claim to have obtained an independent assessment.

Use `methodological-pivots.md` for checks on proxies, missing structure, and implication direction.
Failure of one encoding excludes only that encoding and its proved scope, not an entire discipline.

## Keep Experiments And Side Routes Decision-Relevant

A finite census is appropriate when finite coverage is the user's goal or closes a proved finite
reduction. Otherwise specify which hypotheses the test distinguishes and how each outcome affects
investment. Stop extending a range when it can only repeat existing evidence. Do not mandate finding
the smallest counterexample unless minimality affects the target, explanation, or next test.

After a false claim, return to its role in the proof. A repaired conjecture needs both a mathematical
reason and a surviving implication toward the goal. Repeatedly adding exceptions can turn a useful
claim into a true but irrelevant one.

Record a failed route once, by mechanism and scope, with the evidence required to reopen it. A small
variant is worth a new pass only if it tests an unresolved distinction. Parked routes remain available
when that evidence arrives; parking is a resource decision, not a theorem of impossibility.

## Independent Evidence And Delegation

Use subagents only when authorized. Give them bounded tasks whose results could change the decision:
for example a proof attempt versus a counterexample search, or an independent check of a central
inference. Divide by reasoning mechanism or evidence, not neighbouring cases of the same taxonomy.

A packet needs the exact target and allowed choices, definitions, supplied proved inputs, first
unproved implication, key counterexamples, expected output, effort limit, and review criteria.
Compare assignments by their first unproved implications and evidence roles, not their titles or
technique vocabulary. Merge duplicate construction work or assign a distinct audit/application;
count a shared result once while retaining distinct extensions. Use fewer workers when fewer
independent tasks are justified. Give each worker only the relevant source links; the supervisor
owns cross-campaign synthesis.
For a fresh perspective, omit the detailed route narrative at first; reconcile the result with
archived failures before investing further. Separate
strategy generation from adversarial review rather than asking every agent to elaborate the favoured
route. After a low-yield batch, review the decomposition before running another batch.

## Audit The Skills As Well As The Campaign

When a failure recurs, first determine whether the relevant rule was absent, ambiguous, contradicted
elsewhere, or present but not followed. More prose fixes only some of these problems. Replace or
consolidate the responsible rule; do not append another retrospective checklist by default.

Test a revision against contrasting decisions: a useful central lemma, an irrelevant true lemma,
a repeated failed shortcut, a promising but unfinished proof, a general theory with an unknown return
to the conjecture, a niche family presented as general theory, a prerequisite failure, a correctness
bug, and an explicitly requested finite census. Check that the revision changes the bad decisions
without suppressing the good work. Distinguish structural validation, manual scenario review, and
independent forward testing. None alone demonstrates faster conjecture resolution.
