# Strategy Reviews, Pivots, And State Compression

Use at route selection, plateaus, or checkpoints. Keep reviews inside the
existing ledger rather than creating a second tracking file.

## What Counts As Progress

Separate **contribution** (what statement/capability changed), **confidence**
(author proof / independent audit / exact certificate / numerical evidence /
unresolved), and **effect on target or theory objective**:

| Outcome | Required evidence | Accounting effect |
|---|---|---|
| Target advance | Essential obligation closed, sufficient route loses an obstacle, requested coverage grows, or target bound improves. | Update last goal advance and remaining gap. |
| Reusable theory | General construction, calculus, representation, or comparison adds capability on a natural class. | Record under theory advance; keep target status separate. |
| Decision learning | Live route excluded cleanly or exact reformulation simplifies the essential step. | Revise route choice; keep time since last target advance visible. |
| Local / maintenance | Valid small special case, diagnostic, bug fix, or cleanup with no target/theory impact. | Keep compact; never count as a major advance. |

## Work Backward & Audit Before Extending

Compare 2–3 genuinely different approaches at checkpoints: what gap remains if it
succeeds, why the hardest step should hold, and the cheapest discriminating test.
Check these recurring traps before adding lemmas:

- **Lossy bound vs. false target:** when `bound / truth` grows, test whether an
  adversary breaks the target itself or only the bound (`sum |piece|` destroying
  signed cancellation, or an intermediate proxy growing faster than the target).
- **Exact dual before sufficient proxy:** express the target directly (kernel,
  separator, universal property, optimization dual) and label each route
  `equivalent`, `necessary`, `sufficient`, `special-case`, or `heuristic`. Never
  treat a fixed-choice failure as impossibility when the target allows reselection.
- **Native-structure audit:** list what the target depends on (signs, colors,
  paired layers, endpoint provenance, global compatibility) vs. what a proposed
  import/proxy forgets. If two objects match on retained data with opposite
  target status, that representation alone cannot characterize the target.
- **Local-to-global / input-existence gate:** for $H(X) \land A(X,d) \Rightarrow W(X,F(X,d))$,
  separately verify $H(X) \Rightarrow \exists d\,A(X,d)$—separately satisfiable
  local conditions need not assemble into a globally compatible tuple, and
  induction on smaller instances does not automatically supply prescribed
  boundary values.
- **Stronger-statement quarantine:** when a method secretly pursues a stronger
  sufficient claim than the target, record its scope (`stronger open problem`,
  `false proxy`, `calibration only`, `superseded`) so its failure never falsely
  discredits the weaker original target.

## Anti-Micro-Case Circuit Breaker

Hair-splitting and special-case accretion are a top campaign failure mode:

- **Before taking another special case** (or incrementing $n$, dimension, or
  branch order by 1): name the exact structural obstruction it resolves that a
  uniform argument cannot yet handle.
- **Census-to-principle rule:** unless complete finite coverage was explicitly
  requested or closes a proved finite reduction, freeze a census once it merely
  confirms small instances and extract the structural invariant, transfer lemma,
  or counterexample profile instead.
- **2-wave freeze:** if **2 consecutive rounds or swarm waves** produce only
  special cases, local refinements, or micro-variants without closing the main
  implication, **stop casework** and pivot:
  1. inspect the closest solved analogue or target dual directly;
  2. assign red-team / adversary optimization to falsify the shared hypothesis;
  3. use `alon-method` (external technique dictionary) or `theory-building`
     (common structural law unifying existing partial cases).

## Cumulative Route Review & Short Problem-Packet Restart

Count effort against the same unresolved implication across renamings, workers,
and compactions. Review every 4 attempts (earlier on failure or 2-wave freeze):

1. What changed in completion obligations or reusable theory?
2. Is the mechanism becoming more tractable, or only adding auxiliary debt?
3. Would we start this step today with zero sunk cost?

Output a concrete choice: **Continue** (named new test), **Change mechanism**
(named new structure escaping the old failure), **Pursue independent theory**
(`theory-building` milestone with explicit conjecture status), or **Hold**
(exact reopening condition). Note that sustained `theory-building` programmes use
natural consolidation milestones rather than fixed attempt limits.

When stuck repeatedly at the same obstacle, drop the chronological route story
and draft one 5-line **problem packet** from scratch (original target & exact
quantifiers, definitions & strongest proved facts, distinguishing positive/negative
examples, links to owners, exact remaining implication), design fresh routes from
the target or solved analogue, then cross-check against archived failures.

## Checkpoint Subsumption & Pruning

At every meaningful milestone and before writing up:

- Whenever a unified or stronger theorem is proved, **remove or archive** every
  lemma, module, and dashboard leaf it subsumes; merge redundant files around
  common principles instead of keeping every historical stepping stone live.
- Strip per-iteration chronological counters (e.g., "added in wave 12 / step 7")
  from summaries and dashboards; show clean baseline-vs-current state instead.
- Keep delegation aligned with [swarm-coordination.md](swarm-coordination.md)
  and record outputs using [reporting-and-artifacts.md](reporting-and-artifacts.md).
